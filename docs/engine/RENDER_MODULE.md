# Render module

The renderer and the asset readers of the client rewrite. Imported on 2026-09-14 from the X-Legend map editor of the Server Rework repository, same author, where they draw Gamebryo games of the same era. They replace the home RHI, the HLSL shader system, `nif_import`, `niflib` and `models`.

## Layout

| Folder | Target | Standard | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `engine/formats/` | `KnCFormats` | C++17 | NIF reader 5.0.0.1 to 30.3.0.5, KF and KFM, scene graph, skeleton, animation evaluation, effects and surface state, NIF writer and edit, glTF export. No device |
| `engine/render/` | `KnCRender` | C++20 | bgfx scene renderer, textures, prop and character models, particles, day and night, glow, toon, screenshot callback, NIF to model builder |
| `thirdparty/bgfx/` | `bgfx` `bimg` `bx` | C++20 | bgfx.cmake and its three submodules, pinned |
| `tools/nif/` | `nif_smoke` `nif_rewrite` `nif_export` `nif_animate` `kfm_smoke` | C++17 | Reader checks against a client tree |
| `tools/model_viewer/` | `model_viewer` | C++20 | Opens one NIF in a GLFW window, orbit camera, screenshot switch, clip switch for a skinned character |

Namespaces: `KnC::` for the formats, `KnC::Render` for the renderer. Include paths are repository relative, `engine/formats/nif_reader.h`.

## Backends

bgfx picks the backend at start, `bgfx::RendererType::Count`. Direct3D 11 and 12 and Vulkan on Windows, Vulkan and OpenGL on Linux, Metal on macOS and iOS, Vulkan and OpenGL ES on Android, WebGPU in a browser. The shaders are compiled at configure time for every profile the host can run, `120` glsl, `100_es`, `spirv`, `metal`, and on Windows `s_5_0`, `s_6_0`, `wgsl`. Each is embedded in the library through `BGFX_EMBEDDED_SHADER`.

## Entry points

- `SceneRenderer::init(RendererSetup)` takes a native window handle, a size, an optional `bgfx::CallbackI`, the start hour. It calls `bgfx::init` itself
- `SceneRenderer::upload(const MapScene&)` replaces the whole scene, `append_prop_model`, `set_appended_prop_instances`, `append_character_model`, `update_character_instances` add to a live one
- `SceneRenderer::draw(view, eye)` submits every view, the caller presents with `bgfx::frame()` so an overlay can draw first
- `SceneRenderer::draw_hud_scene(const HudScene&)` draws a small 3D scene into one rect of the frame after `draw` and before the overlay. `HudScene` carries the rect in frame pixels (`x y width height`, origin top left), the camera `view` matrix and its `eye`, the lens `fov_vertical` and `fov_horizontal` (zero follows the rect aspect), `near_plane` `far_plane`, a flat `ambient` the parts take, and `instances`, one `PropInstance` per appended prop model to draw. The rect gets its own bgfx view (`kHudSceneFirstView` and the `kHudSceneViews` after it, four a frame), its depth is cleared inside the rect and the colour kept, no fog and no sun reach it, the particles of the models draw too. `bgfx::setViewOrder` ranks those views before the overlay view 8 so the sprites of the hud land over the scene: the race gauge is `Effect/Guage/red_gauge.nif` through the stock gauge camera (`client/race/GaugeScene.cpp`), the item slot and the minimap may follow
- `SceneRenderer::draw_frame_veil(colour, alpha)` paints a flat colour over the whole frame between the world and the hud scenes, the weather veil of the race, it takes one of the hud scene views
- `SceneRenderer::pose_prop_model_at(model_index, played)` poses an appended model that many seconds into its clips at once, the pools follow on the next frame, the gauge band is posed at the fill times 0.03937 this way. `prop_node_matrix(model_index, name, out)` hands the posed model space matrix of a named `NiNode` (`PropModel::named_nodes`, every named node of the tree with its rest below the nearest animated ancestor), the `O_POS` dummy the gauge spark rides
- `SceneRenderer::animation()` and `day_night()` are the two clocks, the caller advances them
- `set_cel_shading`, `set_glow`, `set_fog`, `set_far_plane`, `set_viewport` are the switches
- `SceneRenderer::set_field_of_view(vertical, horizontal)` is the lens of the frame in radians, the default is `kDefaultFieldOfViewRadians` (60 degrees) and a horizontal of zero derives the width from the viewport aspect. `scene_projection` and the frustum build from the half tangents, passed to bx as near plane extents (bx scales the four extents by the near distance). The race camera sets it every frame, `docs/reverse/CLIENT_CAMERA.md` has the stock rule for the horizontal one. `projection(out)` gives the world projection of the lens so a hud can project world points the way the scene was drawn
- `build_prop_model(NifScene, NifModelRequest, PropModel)` in `nif_prop_model.h` bakes a NIF into one `PropModel`, one part per texture and device state, animated nodes kept as channels, particle systems lifted
- `load_character_model(CharacterModelRequest, CharacterModel&, error)` and `build_character_model(NifScene, request, out, error)` in `nif_character_model.h` turn a skinned NIF plus KF clips into one `CharacterModel`. The request names the NIF, the texture folder and the clips, one `CharacterClipRequest` per KF with an optional name, game id and `sequence_index`, the position of one `NiControllerSequence` inside the KF when the KFM addresses one of several, minus one binds them all. A part is one texture and one device state like a prop part, split again when its bone palette would pass `kBonePaletteSize`, 24 slots. A vertex keeps its geometry space position and up to four palette slots with weights from `NiSkinData`, the four heaviest renormalised. A rigid mesh under a bone, the head of a driver, takes one slot with an identity bind. The rig holds the `NifSkeleton` of the stream, one `BonePalette` per part, skeleton node plus skin to bone bind per slot, and one `CharacterClip` per `NiControllerSequence` of every KF bound by node name. The bounds are the rest pose box. `nif_has_skin(NifScene)` tells a viewer which loader a stream needs
- Clip binding follows the stock `NiControllerSequence::StoreTargets` of `EngineDLL.dll` (0x10081030, the multi target helper 0x1007f5a0): every entry binds to the node of its name, the accumulation root of the sequence only when that node owns a `NiTransformController` in the NIF (`NifSkeletonNode::controlled`), else it keeps its rest transform and counts as unbound. The `main` root of Princess and Yuk has no controller and a 90 degree x rest, the KF poses it to identity and names a `main NonAccum` child the NIF lacks, so binding it laid both bodies on their back. Cosmo binds `Bip01` (its `NiMultiTargetTransformController` chain carries a `NiTransformController`) and the `Bip01 NonAccum` node of the NIF carries the rest back, the same result as the stock
- XYZ rotation keys (`NifKeyInterpolation::XyzRotations`, one float curve per axis) compose as Rz Ry Rx, x first, the NiTransformInterpolator pose quaternion of the same frame proves the order on every driver. Rx Ry Rz turned the Cosmo helmet and the Yuk face 90 degrees and spread the Pumpkin cape wrong
- `TextureCache` turns DDS into textures with its own reader, TGA PNG BMP and JPG through bimg imageParse, one upload per path. The file goes to the device exactly as it was authored, straight alpha, and the fragment hands the device straight alpha too so every blend pair of the `NiAlphaProperty` reads as the artist wrote it. A name the folder does not hold is tried in the game pak through `set_texture_bytes_source`, which `load_track_scene` points at a pak reader of its own unless `TrackSceneRequest::point_textures_at_pak` is off (the client sets it off and points the source at the pak index it read at the start, a second byte scan of the 867 MB pak cost 1 to 43 s at every race entry), then beside itself with another extension or the `_screen` suffix: `Car/Effect/particle_grass.nif` names `GRASS.dds` and this build ships `GRASS_screen.png` only, so the wheel dust on grass drew a white sheet
- `prefetch_textures(paths, threads, stop, background)` reads, parses and inspects the alpha of files on that many threads with no device call, safe off the render thread. The next `TextureCache::acquire` of such a path only creates the GPU texture. `drop_prefetched_textures()` frees what no acquire took, a raised `stop` ends it after the files in hand. A background run puts its helper threads below normal priority (`lower_current_thread_priority()`, a no op off Windows) so a loader that runs while the game runs never takes the cores from the frame loop. `take_texture_load_stats()` hands over the file count and the read, parse, inspect and create ms since the last take, the client prints them per race load step
- `PngScreenshotCallback` copies the frame bgfx was asked to capture and encodes the PNG on its own thread, the render callback no longer stalls the frame (a capture cost 150 to 270 ms of one frame). `finish()` waits for the encoders, `wrote_file()` and `wrote_one_colour()` wait too, and the destructor joins them
- `load_light_rig(path, LightRig, error)` and `apply_light_rig(rig, scene)` in `light_rig.h`, `SceneRenderer::set_sun` changes it live
- `load_track_scene` in `tools/track_scene` keeps the question mark boxes of a race track beside the scene, `TrackScene::item_box_model` from `Item/ItemBox/Type_01/itembox_01.nif` and one `TrackItemBox` per `itembox.ini` row. The model turns and pulses on its own `NiTransformController` chain so nothing is added on top, the caller appends it once and places one instance per spot it wants shown
- A texture name is resolved beside the nif, then `Texture/High` and `Texture/Low` of the map, then a one time index of every `Texture` folder of the whole `World` tree: `Race_01/TRACK.nif` hangs `sn04_badak_04.DDS` of the Snow theme on its wall
- A world space `NiParticleSystem` (`ParticleSystemDefinition::world_space`) of a model placed exactly once is born through that placement, `ParticleSimulation::advance_to` takes the emitter world matrix and `scene_particles.cpp` draws the pool at the world origin, so the puffs stay behind a moving kart. A model placed twice or more keeps the old rule, the pool rides every placement. A world space birth also turns and scales the velocity and the radius by the birth placement and by the emitter world, the dust nif authored at scale 0.036 keeps its size. `restart_prop_particles(model, seconds)` rewinds the pools and the node animation of an appended model, a one shot effect starts from it
- The particle quad samples its sheet with u along the camera right and v down the camera up. Before 2026-09-19 the two corners were swapped so the sheet was transposed, a round puff never showed it but the rain sheet `wait_raindrop01.dds` of `Effect/rain_01.nif`, nine upright streaks on a clear 128 by 128 sheet, drew as flat smears that vanished in the road, the whole rain read as a few specks. `KNC_PARTICLE_DRAW=1` prints the first quad of every pool every 240 frames, its world corner, half extent, colour, material alpha, cutout and draw state, and `KNC_PARTICLE_FLAT=1` paints every pool as opaque white quads (2 the sheet opaque, 3 the sheet blended with no depth test, 4 the sheet blended at full alpha), the two probes that split the geometry from the alpha
- The particle quads are built in world space, the pool point goes through the placement on the CPU and the camera right and up stay world vectors, the quad half extent is the emitter radius times the grow fade size times the uniform scale of the placement times `kRadiusToHalfExtent` 0.3, a rule measured on the stock dust puffs of `docs/client/parity/race_stock.png` at 148 km/h once their gravity let them live (the earlier 0.5 was read on newborn puffs), not read off the exe, the Gamebryo packer was not traced. Before that the quad was built in model space and a placement with a yaw turned it edge on, a kart seen from behind lost its sparks
- A world space pool scales the gravity strength like the velocity, by the birth placement and by the emitter world, the strength of a nif is in its own units. The dust nif authored at 0.036 with a strength of 7200 fell like a rock before, the exe writes 50 to 250 on it and those read as 1.8 to 9 world units a second squared
- `NiPSysEmitterCtlr` carries a second link, the emitter active interpolator, a `NiBoolInterpolator` on a `NiBoolData` key group. The reader keeps it in `NifController::visibility_interpolator_link`, the builder resolves it into `ParticleSystemDefinition::birth_visibility` and `ParticleSimulation::emitter_runs` stops the births while it reads below a half. The podium confetti is a burst gated open from 1.6 s to 4.23 s of its five second window, with the gate skipped it never stopped and the frame carried three times the flakes of the stock
- Every pool seeds its xorshift from its system name, `seed_of`. One shared seed made the three `Snow` systems of the podium drop their flakes at the same points, one stack of three colours per fall instead of a spread of single colours
- `ParticleSystemDefinition::drive` is a shared `ParticleDrive` the placer may fill after the load, `frequency` replaces the `NiTimeController` frequency of the emitter controller and `gravity_strength` the strength of every gravity modifier, the wheel dust of the client writes both per frame from the speed. The emission count runs on `ParticleSimulation::emission_time`, the scaled seconds of the emitter controller, a loop never wraps the count so a frequency of 16 emits sixteen times the authored rate, a clamp holds it at the window end so a one shot burst stops, a reverse walks the window
- `NiFlipController` is read (`NifTextureFlip` in `nif_animation.h`, the map and the `NiSourceTexture` links, `read_flip_controller`), the builder hangs it on a particle system whose base map it drives (`flip_textures`, `flip`, `has_flip`), `refresh_particles` samples the float keys at the played seconds, truncates the value to a file index like Gamebryo and swaps the system texture through the cache. The drift sparks and the dust of the client flip two files at 30 Hz. A flip on a mesh part is built too since 2026-09-19: the builder keeps one `FlipAnimation` per driven `NiTexturingProperty` in `ModelAnimation::flip_channels` (`PartAnimation::flip` names it), `evaluate_model_animation` writes the file index of the frame into `ModelPose::flip_frame` and `bind_prop_part` binds that file (`PropPartBuffers::flip_textures`, resolved beside the base map by `resolve_textures`). The blur sheet of the intro flips `background6` and `background7` at 30 Hz and the gauge band `board_redg01` `board_redg02`
- A `NiMeshParticleSystem` draws the templates of its `NiPSysMeshUpdateModifier` (`NifPSysModifier::mesh_links`, the `NiMeshPSysData` node link is an empty container on the client data and stays as the fallback), the builder walks them, `mesh_particles` is set, `mesh_half_extent` is the widest half extent of the templates in their own space, `mesh_uv_min` `mesh_uv_max` the uv box and `mesh_uv_patches` one box per face, a particle takes one face patch by its pool slot. The quad draws at the template half extent times the particle size, the way Gamebryo scales a mesh particle: the emitter writes its initial radius into the size, the birth placement and the emitter world scale that radius, so the podium templates (1.4 units at scale 0.352, emitter radius 0.7) come out about 0.35 units, ten pixels on the stock podium frame. The earlier rule dropped the particle size and drew them twice too big
- The emitter radius of a newborn is set before the world space birth scales it, the earlier order wrote the nif unit radius after the scale so a world space pool kept it, the dust puffs were 30 unit discs and the confetti 0.7 unit squares at a 0.352 placement
- A shape with no material of its own takes the resolved material of its parents for its alpha channel (`part_animation_of`), the crash sheets of `Effect/crush_B.nif` fade through a `NiAlphaController` on a parent material
- `NiGeomMorpherController` is read (`NifGeomMorpher` and `NifMorphData` in `nif_animation.h`), the builder bakes every `NiMorphData` frame into the part space (`PropPart::morph_frames`, `morph_first_vertex`, `morph_relative`) and keeps one weight channel a frame in `ModelAnimation::morph_channels`. A morphed part owns a `bgfx::DynamicVertexBufferHandle` and `refresh_morphed_parts` writes the posed shape every time a weight moves: the relative form is frame 0 plus the sum of weight times frame i, the absolute form the weighted blend. The mushman of `Forest_02` carries three frames on its body part, the spider of `Swamp_01` and `Swamp_03` the same, and both stood still before
- `NiBillboardNode` is read (`NifBlock::billboard_mode`, `has_billboard`), `billboard_facing` folds the seven wire modes into three shapes, `BillboardFacing::FaceCamera` for 0 and 3, `RotateAboutUp` for 1 5 and 9, `RigidFaceCamera` for 2 and 4. A pose keeps `billboard_owner` per animated node, `node_billboard` and the chain below the billboard node in `below_billboard`, and `placed_matrix` replaces the world rotation of that node with the camera facing before it composes the chain under it. The item box glow plane and the turbo plane keep the authored facing without it
- `KNC_PROP_LOG=1` prints the blend pair, the alpha test and the material alpha of every particle system too, the probe that tells a system with no draw state from one the camera never sees

- `KNC_PART_TINT=1` paints every built part a flat colour of its own index, red plus green times 8 plus blue times 64, a probe that reads the guilty part straight off a picture. It found the black road slab of Pinky Road
- `KNC_PROP_LOG=1` prints every built part of every nif that loads, its texture, vertex and triangle count, model space box, blend pair and alpha test, depth flags, two sided flag, animated node, uv flip and detail channels, uv box, material colours and detail map, every shape the build leaves out and why, every flip channel with its keys, then every particle system with its pool, texture, filter, mesh template half extent, birth rate, emitter gate and modifiers with the emitter colour and launch angles. The probe that found the room floor, the podium confetti and the transposed rain sheet

## Reading the device state

- The base map filter mode of the `NiTexturingProperty` (`NifTextureFilter`, the enum after the clamp on the 10.x layout, bits 0 to 3 of the flags word on 20.1) reaches the sampler through `diffuse_sampler(clamp, filter)`: nearest and bilerp name no mip level so `TextureCache::acquire(path, mipmapped)` uploads the top level alone and the sampler takes point mips, the other modes keep the chain. Every KnC particle sheet and part read trilerp, the field was read and dropped before
- The detail map of slot 2 of the `NiTexturingProperty` (`NifBlock::detail_texture_link` and its matrix, `PropPart::detail_texture_path`, `PartAnimation::detail_uv` for the `NiTextureTransformController` that names map 2) draws as the D3D9 fixed function second stage does, MODULATE2X: `fs_scene.sc` multiplies the base texel by the detail texel and doubles it when `u_detailParams.x` is set, sampler 2 `s_detail` with its own `u_detailRows` `u_detailOffset` matrix. The fill band of `red_gauge.nif` is a grey base `board_redg01.DDS` under the scrolling orange detail `board_redg02.dds`, it drew flat grey before
- Every pair of the `NiAlphaProperty` flags reaches the device, `blend_state` in `scene_draw_state.h` sets `BGFX_STATE_BLEND_FUNC(source, destination)` from bits 1 to 4 and 5 to 8. The old fragment was premultiplied so it forced the source factor to ONE and printed a warning for the rest, which drew the charged drift sparks `Car/Effect/DRIFT2.nif` (source colour, destination colour) as a light blue sheet, the exhaust smoke and the water dust (destination colour, source alpha) the same way
- The alpha test function of bits 10 to 12 reaches the shader through the sign of the cutout in `u_splatParams.z`: above zero it drops a texel under the value, below zero it drops one at or over minus the value, and a GREATER test takes half a step more so a reference of 0 still drops the clear texels. That last half step is what clears the black square around every effect sprite, the spark systems test GREATER 0 and a plain 0 cutout tested nothing
- A surface whose mesh carries no normals takes no sun at all. `shade.sh` measures the normal first and skips the light when it is zero: `normalize` of a zero vector is a NaN and the whole lit colour collapsed to black, so the road slab of `Forest/Forest_01/track.nif` and one wall of most tracks painted flat black across the frame. The device leaves a stream without a normal unlit in the same way
- A geometry wearing bit 0 of the `NiAVObject` flags word, the app culled bit, is not built. The mushman of `Forest_02` keeps a second body under its own and the exe shows one at a time

- `NiZBufferProperty` below stream 20.1.0.2 keeps only the test and the write bit in its flags word, the test function is a separate 32 bit enum after it. The reader read the flags and threw that enum away, so `test_function()` read bits 2 to 4 of a flags word that never holds them, gave function 0 ALWAYS, and every part of every KnC nif (they are all 10.2.0.0) drew with `BGFX_STATE_DEPTH_TEST_ALWAYS`. Draw order then decided the picture: the room floor `World/Room/Floor/Floor01/track.nif` painted its clover ground, the last part of the file, over its own trees, fence and flowers, and a kart body painted its far side over its near side. `read_z_buffer_property` now folds that enum into bits 2 to 4 so both layouts read the same way. The rewrite pass is unchanged, the writer copies raw bytes
- A blended part that also writes depth takes a one step alpha cutout (`kClearTexelCutout`, 1/255) so a clear texel neither paints nor writes depth. Without it the wing quads of `Effect/Podium/winer_02.nif` wrote depth over the sign plate behind them and the road showed through their transparent corners

## Shading

The fragment shader is the fixed function model measured on the X-Legend client: emissive plus ambient times the ambient colour, alpha from the vertex or the material, no directional light, sampler clamp from the texture flags, back to front sort of the translucent parts. The measurements were made on HBOnline.exe. KnC adds one light, read in `docs/reverse/CLIENT_SHADING.md`: the world loads `Data/Public/World/Light.nif` once per track, one NiAmbientLight at 200 200 200 and one NiDirectionalLight at 255 246 235. `light_rig.h` reads that file, `apply_light_rig` puts the ambient into the scene table and the sun into `MapScene::sun`, the vertex shaders add diffuse times the sun colour times the lambert term of the world normal, the diffuse comes from the material or the vertex colour like the ambient. The light shines along its node X axis, flipped to travel down since the data does not settle the sign. A scene without a sun draws as before. Lights embedded in car and track NIFs are exporter leftovers and stay ignored.

`load_track_scene` in `tools/track_scene` also keeps one `TrackGimmick` per placed themed gimmick, the world spot and the `GimmickClass` of `games/kart/physics/client/gimmicks.h` the client hit test of that name carries. The identity loads take the rest bound centre of their nif, the mushman and the spider rows take the tables baked in the exe. The race screen turns the list into the `GimmickWorld` that `world_gimmick_hit_dispatch` 0x4D3950 sweeps.

The sky nif is loaded by the stock like the track nif, `world_track_init` 0x487900 calls the same `scene_holder_load_nif` for `World/%s/%s/sky`, so the dome stands still where it was authored and never follows the camera. Every dome hangs under a node with a big negative z, `Palace_01` at 18.5 290.8 minus 65.5 with its shape another 269.7 down, so a camera locked dome put the horizon at eye height and filled the frame with the dark underside. `submit_sky` now draws at the identity and `submit_sky_part` composes the animated node like a prop part, and the sky depth range takes twice the dome radius plus the headroom since the camera sits well off its centre. `SceneRenderer::set_sky_phase(index)` picks the phase, minus one leaves it to the hour of the clock: the stock has no hour, `world_track_init` reads the flag at 0xB2319C, which the `0x0014` launch packet writes at offset 0x14, and loads `sky_night` when it is 1 with `sky` as the fallback.

Glow is the client post process, 128 by 128 downsample, bright pass, two blurs, additive combine. Toon shading with an outline pass exists and is off by default.

The translucent accumulator sorts blended parts far to near by the depth of their world bound centre, as NiAlphaAccumulator does by default. Sorting by the nearest point of the sphere made a big box draw after a small sign on its face and paint over it. The depth is minus the dot with the third view axis: a right handed `bx::mtxLookAt` stores the axis that looks back at the eye there, and until 2026-09-19 the key was the plain dot so the queue ran near to far. Every blended part that writes depth then hid what stood behind it: the `Login_THA_00` wallpaper of the intro, clear on its alpha controller for six seconds, wrote depth over the sky plates and the `background6` blur sheet painted its streaks over a black hole, the two plates the intro had to drop. With the order right the intro shows its 48 surfaces and the clear texel cutout of a depth writing blended part is a safety net, not the fix. A `NiStencilProperty` with draw mode 3 (both sides) sets `NifSurfaceState::two_sided` and the part draws with no cull, the reader keeps the draw mode in `NifBlock::stencil_draw_mode` for the 10.x layout and the 20.1 flags word.

The frame is the KnC one, z up, right handed, the wire and start.ini space without any transform. The projection is built with `bx::Handedness::Right` and the view matrices of the viewers too, closed surfaces cull `BGFX_STATE_CULL_CW`, the silhouette shell the other way. The test is the CHIBI KART banner over the Race 01 start, a left handed projection mirrors its text.

## Checking it

```
release\model_viewer.exe "<client>\Data\Public\Car\Body\High\Basic_1\BODY.nif"
release\model_viewer.exe <file.nif> --screenshot out.png --frames 3
release\model_viewer.exe <file.nif> --textures <dir> --no-sun
release\model_viewer.exe "<client>\Data\Public\Driver\Body\High\Cosmo\body.nif" --clip "<client>\Data\Public\Driver\Body\High\Cosmo\body_O_MAIN_MT_IDLE.kf" --screenshot out.png --frames 30
release\dat_manager.exe --game <client> --open Data/Public/Driver/Body/High/Princess/body.nif --screenshot out.png --frames 30 --view 0,-0.15
release\nif_smoke.exe <client>\Data
```

The dat manager line draws one driver of the pak on its MT_IDLE clip from the front, `--view yaw,pitch` sets the orbit in radians (yaw 0 stands on the minus x side, the face of a driver, 1.57 the left side). Every driver of the pak captured that way front and side is `docs/client/parity/drivers_ours.png`, all ten upright and facing minus x.

The viewer finds `Public/World/Light.nif` above the NIF and lights the scene with it, `--no-sun` shows the ambient only model, `--textures` points at a BODYCOLOR folder for a kart colour. `nif_smoke` walks a tree and reports every block it could not decode. `nif_rewrite` reads and writes every file and diffs the bytes. The screenshot switch opens no visible window, a capture that is one flat colour is reported as such.

A NIF with skin blocks, or any NIF given `--clip`, goes through `load_character_model` instead of the prop builder and is uploaded as a character. The first clip plays on the animation clock from the start, `--clip` may repeat to bind more. `SceneRenderer::set_character_clip(index)` switches every character of the scene to another clip, minus one hands each instance back its own `CharacterInstance::clip`, and `character_clips()` lists what one character scene holds. The Cosmo line above is the check of the loader, the driver must come out seated in the idle pose with its head and body textured.

## What goes

The old RHI based stack (rhi, shaders, graphics, renderer, nif_import, niflib, models) and its consumers (the old kart game layer, engine visual tests, demos) are not part of this repository. `engine/render` and `engine/formats` are the only render stack shipped here. Every tool draws through this module: `tools/map_viewer`, `tools/model_viewer`, `tools/ui_editor` and `tools/dat_manager`.

## Origin

Server Rework commit e0382186, `core/formats` and `tools/xlegend-map/render` of that repository. Not imported: the X-Legend terrain, path map and grid readers, the table reader, the editor shell, the document layer and the live server bindings. `nif_prop_model.cpp` is the editor's prop mesh builder with the X-Legend node filters removed.
