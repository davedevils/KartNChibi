## S2C GhostFrameCount and 0x00AE C2S ReplayUploadCount

```
+0x00  u32  frame_count
size   4 in BOTH directions
```

S2C targets ghost car slot 1 and resets the append cursor. MUST be sent BEFORE 0xAA.
Nothing else ever clears the frame count, so a stale ghost survives stage transitions.
Always send it even with 0.
C2S is the start of the replay upload from player car slot 0. The client hard clamps its
own count to 2399, anything above is forged. No ack, the client goes straight to 0xAF.
