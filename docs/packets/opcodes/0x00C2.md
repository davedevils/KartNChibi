0x01A2A748, cap 512, stride 0xDC, count at container+0x1B804. Lookups `part_catalog_lookup` 0x4510C0

Here the wire order IS the record order. Note the first string sits BETWEEN the two u32 groups.
There is no stat block in this record, the 17 part bonus floats live in the 0x0108
CarCraftPartDefinition record at +0x78 (container stride 0x120, `part_def_record_lookup` 0x44FBC0),
that is what `stat_bonus_add_part` 0x48F710 reads. Migration 013 said word_0c carried the category,
the client reads +0x0C as the required level and +0x38 as the slot, the live sender
PacketBuilder::partCatalog already writes it that way.

## Our server

`GameServerLoginBurst` sends one 0x00C2 per `def_kart_skin` row through
`PacketBuilder::partCatalog`, and the price option list comes from `shop_option` category 3.

The recording sells 367 of its 473 part rows. Every sold row carries exactly three options in
this order, one day, seven days, permanent. The tier is picked by the equip slot at record
+0x38: slots 2 to 6, the parts the driver wears, use 2003 3003 1003, and slots 0 kart colour,
1 name plate and 8 kart item use 2004 3004 1004. The other 106 rows ship visible 0 with one
inert option whose price key is 0. Every part row of the recording carries required level 0
and badge 0.

Until migration 062 our `shop_definition` held category 3 rows only for the 57 kart parts
9001 to 9328, while `shop_option` already had 1463 rows for 457 part keys. So the tile drew
and the client resolved a price, but `ShopPackets::validatePurchase` answered
`UnknownDefinition` for every driver accessory and the client printed MSG_UNKNOWN_ERROR.
Migration 062 seeds one definition row per `def_kart_skin` key, visible where the key has an
option and 0 where it has none, subtype set to the skin family and level 0 like the recording.
The 30 default driver costume parts 9201 to 9230 and the stock plate 9100 have no option, so
they stay not visible and are never sold, which is what the recording does with its own
unsold rows.

### After the audit of 2026-09-23

Migration 068 puts every sold row on the burst tier of its slot, 2003 3003 1003 for slots 2 to 6 and 2004 3004 1004
for slots 0 1 8, with the unit and amount in the option words, adds the War Flag `ant_19` (burst key 1219, sold) as
9329, and moves the stock plate 9100 `NAMEBOX_NORMAL` from slot 7 to slot 1 like the burst key 1100, still hidden.
The login burst no longer ships the car craft twin rows on keys 1000 to 7004: keys 2000 to 2004 of the twins are
`common_char_head_001` to `005`, `part_catalog_lookup` found the twin first and the five heads could not render,
and no reader needs a twin, see [0x0108](0x0108.md). The Emerald Tiara (burst key 14213, sold) stays out, the
client ships no nif for it. Needs the new image for the twins and 068 for the rows.
