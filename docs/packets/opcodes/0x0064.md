# 0x0064

| Direction | Name | Client fn | Server symbol | Size | System | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S2C | RoomTeamUpdate | sub_47ACD0 | PacketBuilder::roomStatus | 8 | room teams | PROVEN |
| C2S | RoomTeamSelect | sub_4816A0 | GameServer::handleTeamChange | 4 | room teams | PROVEN |

## S2C RoomTeamUpdate and 0x0064 C2S RoomTeamSelect

```
S2C  +0x00 u32 player_id, +0x04 u32 team   size 8
C2S  +0x00 u32 team                         size 4
```

Team 0 increments dword_BCE238 and decrements dword_BCE23C, team 1 the reverse.
Any other value writes the slot and leaves both counters alone.
Only two C2S call sites and both pass a literal 0 or 1.

## Our server

`GameServerDispatch.cpp` routes C2S 0x64 on the session state. In a room it is the team
select and goes to `GameServer::handleTeamChange` in
`server/game/src/GameServerRaceHandlers.cpp`, which keeps the wire space, 0 red 1 blue,
maps it to the room side, 1 red 2 blue, refuses a side already at half the cap, and
broadcasts the S2C 0x64 `PacketBuilder::roomStatus(player_id, team)` with the wire value.

Outside a room the same int32 is read as a game mode, `LobbyHandler::handleQuickMatch` in
`server/game/src/handlers/LobbyHandler.cpp` hands it to `GameServer::quickMatch`. That is
the quick match of our own client only. The stock client never sends 0x64 from the lobby,
its two senders sit in the room stage `FUN_0040D9D0`, and its quick buttons send 0x2C.
Both routes end in the same room chain, see [0x002C](0x002C.md).

The team value here and the one at +0x04 of [0x0021](0x0021.md) are the same space.
`wireTeam` in `server/game/include/GameServerInternal.h` converts the room side to it
for every 0x21, 0x3E and scoreboard row, so a red member counts red on both packets.
