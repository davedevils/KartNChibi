## S2C RoomMemberJoin

```
+0x00  u32           slot          MUST be 0..29, NO range check in the client
+0x04  u32           team
+0x08  u32           player_id
+0x0C  UTF16LE wstr  display_name  the ONLY wide field, 28 byte slot, practical 12 wchars
 L+0   u8            level_index
 L+1   u8            gm_badge_selector
 L+2   u8            pccafe_projection
 L+3   u32           equipped_title_key    NO padding before it
 L+7   blob[0x2C]    character_record
 L+0x33 blob[0x38]   kart_record
 L+0x6B u32          ready_state
 L+0x6F u32          pet_base_key
 L+0x73 blob[0x3C]   custom_car_block
size   187+2*(name+1),  L = 0x0C+2*(name+1)
```

READ ORDER IS 4,4,4,wstr,1,1,1,4,0x2C,0x38,4,4,0x3C. ready_state comes BEFORE pet_base_key
on the wire even though the commit fn takes it last.
Guards in `sub_40D650` in order:
1. room init flag at room+0x1CDD8, only the 0x0013 stage 9 enter sets it
2. slot not already occupied
3. slot+0x6A20 ENABLED non zero, which is the 0x0032 guard
4. `sub_450060(driver catalog, character[+0x04])` non zero
5. `sub_44F6F0(kart catalog, kart[+0x04])` non zero
Slot 30 corrupts the spectator array and the init flag. Server MUST clamp.

## Our server, where the two blobs come from

`character_record` and `kart_record` are the SAME records the owned lists published, not
rebuilt ones. Their field maps live on the list pages, `blob[0x2C]` is the record of
[0x001B](0x001B.md) and `blob[0x38]` is the record of [0x001C](0x001C.md).

So the two keys guard 4 and 5 resolve, character+0x04 against the 0xBF driver catalogue and
kart+0x04 against the 0xC0 kart catalogue, are `driver_base_key` at 0x001B record +0x04 and
`kart_base_key` at 0x001C record +0x04. A member whose owned row carries a base key that was
never sent in a catalogue packet is refused by `sub_40D650` and never appears in the room.

`buildRoomMember` in `server/game/src/GameServerRoomHandlers.cpp` builds the wire.
It calls `InventoryHandler::selectedCharacterRow` and `selectedKartRow` for the active owned
rows, then `InventoryPackets::characterBlob` and `InventoryPackets::kartBlob` in
`server/game/src/packets/gen/InventoryPackets.cpp`, which are the same two functions
`InventoryPackets::ownedCharacterList` and `ownedKartList` call for 0x1B and 0x1C. One
builder, so the three packets can never disagree. A missing owned row falls back to
`PacketBuilder::characterRecord` from the driver table and loses the equipped accessories,
and that fallback is logged as an error.

The team at +0x04 is the wire space of [0x0064](0x0064.md), 0 red 1 blue. `sub_40D650`
counts `param_3 == 0` into the red counter `0xBCE238` and `== 1` into the blue one
`0xBCE23C` in modes 1 and 3, the same two counters the 0x64 handler moves. The room stores
0 none 1 red 2 blue, `wireTeam` in `server/game/include/GameServerInternal.h` converts it
inside `buildRoomMember` and `buildBotMember`, so a red member is 0 here and 0 on 0x64.
Before that fix a red member went out as 1 and counted blue. In team modes
`Room::addPlayer` and `Room::addBots` seat every newcomer on the smaller side, red on a
tie, so what the client counts from these frames is what the server holds.

Order inside the room chain of [0x0013](0x0013.md): every other human by slot, the
receiver last, then the CPU cars, each after its 0x32.

## The seat rule, one member one slot

Guard 2 above is why the server may never hand the same slot to two members. `Room::addPlayer`
and `Room::addBots` in `shared/src/src/game/Room.cpp` both take their seat from
`Room::nextFreeSlot`, which scans the humans and the CPU cars together over 0 to the room cap
and returns `Room::kNoSlot` when nothing is free, and both refuse rather than reuse a slot.
Before that fix `findEmptySlot` looked at the humans alone, so a human joining a room that
already held a bot took the bot's slot. The client then dropped one of the two 0x21 frames while
the race still spawned both cars on [0x003E](0x003E.md), which has no slot guard, and the room
showed one CPU car less than the race. `tests/server/test_room_bots.cpp` pins it.

## The u32 after the pccafe byte is the worn pendant

`sub_499180` reads member plus 0x6A50 and draws `Icon/<base>_s` left of the room name plate.
Since 2026-09-23 (needs the new image) `buildRoomMember` fills it with
`characters.pendant_key`, it went out as zero. Our client keeps it on `RoomMember::pendantKey`.
