`ProgressionHandler::grantEarnedPendants` grants every pendant a character has earned and the live grant rides
here: 01 at licence grade 1 or more, 02 to 05 when the quest stages 5 10 15 20 are completed
(`scenario_key_progress`, `questPendantFor`), 06 07 at 100 and 1000 races, 08 to 12 at level 10 to 50, and the
hidden 13 when the five missions of chapter 1 are cleared (`char_mission_progress` 0 to 4). It runs at login, after
a race, after a level up, after a first mission clear, after a licence pass and after a rewarded quest. Hidden
rows are granted too. Migration 068 drops the pendant 01 reward of mission 2 and the 01 rows of characters with
no licence grade. Needs the new image.
