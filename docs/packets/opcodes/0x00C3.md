## S2C TrackDefinition

Handler `track_catalog_recv_0xc3` 0x47F990, store `track_catalog_store` 0x453020 into the container
0x01A45F50, cap 128, stride 0x8C, count at container+0x4604. Lookups `track_catalog_lookup` 0x4531F0
by track id at rec+0x04, `track_catalog_by_index` 0x453070 (flag mode skips rec+0x00 zero),
`track_catalog_count` 0x453100, `track_catalog_first_id_of_theme` 0x453140. `world_track_init`
0x4875C0 keeps the resolved record at world+0x1334C and the global 0x01AF2B5C, every reader of those
two pointers was checked too (tick, respawn, rally, gimmicks, minimap, lens flare).

Read order is strict: u32, u32, u32, cstr, 14 x u32, cstr. An unknown folder fails the track load
with `Track initialize fail`. Our track_catalog rows hold 0 in fog_far, the client then sets a far
clip of 0 and the engine ignores it, the room world uses 5000 and a real capture should fill it.
They hold 0 0 0 in lens_flare_x y z too, the stock aims its flare at the world origin then, the clone
client reads the three into `TrackRow::lensFlare` and keeps its flare off for an all zero point.
