## S2C ItemDrumPicked and 0x00A1 C2S ItemDrumPickup

```
+0x00  u32  item_drum_index
size   4 in BOTH directions
```

NOT A MISSION OPCODE. Bounds checked against the per track drum table, requires the entry
to be spawned, sets taken, stamps now_ms and fires a pickup effect.
The table comes from the track loader, not from the network, max 80 entries.
The client takes the pickup LOCALLY first then reports it, at 20 units range.
Relay to the OTHER clients only.
