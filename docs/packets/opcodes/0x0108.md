## Our server

`CustomCarPackets::partDef` builds one frame per row of `carcraft_part_def`.
`CarCraftHandler::sendPartDefs` sends the whole table once per session and skips a
second burst, so the blind client append never doubles a row.

Until 2026-09-18 the login burst shipped the same 35 rows a second time through
`PacketBuilder::carPartCatalog`, so the headless saw 70 frames where the recorded burst
carries 35 and every car craft part stood twice in the shop tab and in the factory strip.
That loop is gone, `sendLoginSnapshot` is the only sender and `PacketBuilder::partCatalog`
keeps shipping the 0x00C2 twin row the model builder needs. The burst copy also wrote the
part key into +0x00 and +0x04 where the recording has zeros, and its price rows came from
`shop_option` while the typed builder reads `carcraft_part_price`, so the two copies of one
part disagreed on the price.

The 35 recorded rows are keys 7000 to 7136 step 4 and every one carries the same three price
rows, 2007 one day, 3007 seven days, 1007 permanent, the fourth dword of a price row is 0.
Ours keep the emulator keys 1000 to 7004 and migration 061 points them at the same three
price keys, since the old `carcraft_part_price` rows named 7001000 and friends which no
0x00C6 row ever carried, so `price_row_lookup` failed and the tile was skipped.

### The ability block and the twins, 2026-09-23

The pair block at record +0xBC is read: `carcraft_part_ability_pairs_draw` 0x42B7A0 walks two pairs at +0xBC and
+0xC4 and draws an icon and `%d%%` for an id 0 to 25, like the kart and part pairs. The burst sends
`FF FF FF FF 00 00 00 00` twice, the package image sent zeros so every part tile drew two `0%` icons.
`CustomCarPackets::partDef` now writes `CarPartDef::abilityId` (default -1) and `abilityPercent`. The tail triple
at +0xCC is the tires row copied into car+0xA7988 by `car_apply_kart_loadout`, +0xD4 adds to the wheel grip, 0 on
both servers. The 0x00C2 twin rows are gone from the login burst, the factory branch of `car_apply_kart_loadout`
and `FUN_004A5ED0` take the parts from this container only. The record rules of stage 18 are checked by
`tests/server/test_client_reader_rules.cpp`, see [../SHOP_CATALOGUE.md](../SHOP_CATALOGUE.md). Needs the new image.
