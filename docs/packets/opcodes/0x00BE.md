## S2C ClearAllCatalogs

```
payload none, any payload is accepted and discarded
```

NOT race init. Resets 20 containers in a row including the driver catalog, the mission
definitions and the notes. It does NOT clear the 0xA2 license progress list nor the 0x88
mission progress list.
MUST be sent BEFORE the 0xBF burst and NEVER after. Sending it after wipes the driver
catalog and the next S2C 0x0003 divides by the entry count and crashes.
