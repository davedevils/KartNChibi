## S2C DespawnRacer

```
+0x00  u32  player_id
size   4
```

Removes car and driver, then decrements the 0x14 player_count UNLESS the 0x14 game_mode is
1 or 3. No C2S 0x3F exists, the real join is C2S 0x2F.

## Our server

Never sent from a lobby or room path. The `PacketBuilder::roomInfo` builder that wrote a
room id here is gone with the 0x64 lobby quick match that used it. A room enter is the
[0x0013](0x0013.md) chain.
