# 0x0047

| Direction | Name | Client fn | Server symbol | Size | System | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S2C | ItemEffectSpawn | sub_47A110 | *ItemPackets::itemSpawn | 24 | items | PROVEN |
| C2S | ItemUseRequest | sub_481230 | *ItemPackets::parseUse | 20 | items | PROVEN |

## S2C ItemEffectSpawn

```
+0x00  u32       player_id
+0x04  i32       effect_kind
+0x08  f32/u32   p1   spawn_x
+0x0C  f32/u32   p2   spawn_y
+0x10  f32/u32   p3   spawn_z
+0x14  f32/u32   p4   yaw_deg
size   24
```

DANGER: only player_id is read BEFORE the car lookup, the other five dwords are read INSIDE
the guard. An unknown id leaves 20 bytes unconsumed and desyncs the frame.
Kinds with a case: 2 3 4 5 7 8 9 11 12 13 14 15 17 18 19 20 21.
Kinds 2 3 7 9 11 13 14 15 18 19 read p1..p4 as f32 world coords, the rest ignore but still
consume them. Must be echoed TO THE SENDER TOO for kinds 2 3 5 8 9 11 14 15 17 18 19 20.

## C2S ItemUseRequest

```
+0x00  i32  item_kind
+0x04  f32  spawn_x
+0x08  f32  spawn_y
+0x0C  f32  spawn_z
+0x10  f32  yaw_deg
size   20
```

No player_id. The server must PREPEND the sender id and re emit the 24 byte S2C 0x47.

## Our server

`RaceHandler::handleItemSpawn` in `server/game/src/handlers/RaceHandler.cpp`, routed from
case 0x47 in `server/game/src/GameServerDispatch.cpp`, is the whole path. In order:

1. drop unless the room is Racing, and drop a `kind` outside 0..21 (`itemKindValid`)
2. drop when the sender is not on the race roster, the receiver bails mid stream on an
   unknown id and loses the other 20 bytes
3. rate gate, two uses closer than `ITEM_USE_MIN_INTERVAL_MS = 250` bump `suspiciousCount`
   and the second one is dropped
4. broadcast S2C 0xC9 sound cue to everyone EXCEPT the sender, the receiver plays it with no
   per kind switch
5. drop with a log when `spawnHasClientCase(kind)` is false, kinds 0 1 6 10 16 have no
   receiver case at all
6. remember it as a bot hazard when the kind maps to an effect, see the table below
7. rebuild the 24 byte S2C 0x47 with our own sender id prepended, `ItemPackets::itemSpawnEcho`

WHO GETS THE ECHO is decided by `ItemPackets::spawnNeedsSelfEcho` in
`server/game/src/packets/gen/ItemPackets.cpp`, which is `spawnHasClientCase(kind) &&
!spawnAppliesLocally(kind)`:

```
spawnHasClientCase  2 3 4 5 7 8 9 11 12 13 14 15 17 18 19 20 21
spawnAppliesLocally 0 1 10 12 16 21     the shooter already spawned it itself
self echo           2 3 4 5 7 8 9 11 13 14 15 17 18 19 20      broadcast to all
no self echo        12 and 21           broadcastExcept the sender, a second one would double
```

Our set is wider than the one above, we also echo kinds 4, 7 and 13 back to the shooter.
The rule we apply is derived, has a receiver case and does not apply locally, not a
per kind list read out of the binary.

WHICH SPAWN HITS WHOM. A dropped item is a hazard only for the CPU cars, our server never
resolves a human hit from a spawn, the victim reports it with C2S 0x69.
`hazardEffect` in `server/game/src/handlers/RaceHandler.cpp` maps the kind to the effect
the bot takes:

```
2  SPIKE -> 100 spin      18 BOMB -> 300 crash     14 ICE  -> 1000 ice
11 HIVE  -> 700 hive      19 DUNG -> 100 spin      everything else, no hazard
```

`RaceHandler::noteHazard` stores it with the owner id and a timestamp, and `tickBots` arms
it after 400 ms, keeps it live for 25000 ms, gives the owner a 2000 ms immunity to its own
drop, and triggers on a 3.0 world unit reach. A bot that drives into one takes the effect
and the server broadcasts S2C 0x69 for it.

`server/game/src/handlers/ItemHandler.cpp` used to hold a second implementation of this
handler that nothing routed to. It is gone, the file keeps only the two wired handlers,
`handleAbilityClass` for 0xF2 and `handleAbilityFire` for 0xCD.
